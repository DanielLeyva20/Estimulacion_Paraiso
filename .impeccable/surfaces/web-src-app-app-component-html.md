# Landing — Mi Paraíso

Scope: single landing page (Angular app root). Mode: Persuade.
Audience: parents of 0–3 year olds in Imperial, Cañete, on phones. Action: WhatsApp (51974987291).
User additions (binding): organized, orderly landing structure; appropriate, highly legible typography; enough information that parents understand what is offered and feel safe.
Proof/content: reference photos only (Unsplash, replaceable); no prices, schedules, address, staff, testimonials or stats — WhatsApp answers those.
Unresolved: real photos, exact address, hours, prices, safety facts to confirm.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Mi Paraíso is a small valley seen from above — each space and program is a plot (parcela) of pastel field, stitched by a single irrigation channel (acequia) that carries the visitor from plot to plot. Refuses the category default of blob shapes, cartoon clouds and identical icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: Field tints on a pale adobe-sand ground: cotton/sand #F7F1E6, young-maize mint #D4EAD9, camote blush #F7D9D3, ripe-grain butter #F7E6A6, river sky #CFE2F2, lilac vine #E3DAF0, deep furrow ink #2F3B33. Plots: flat color fields with 22px radius on one module grid, photos set as whole plots. The acequia: one 3px river-blue stroke with soft flow dashes. Type: M PLUS Rounded 1c 800 display, Onest body.

STORY: See the place (spaces), understand what they do (programs, ages), trust them (how we care, parents welcome, how to start, FAQ), write on WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: left 5/12 headline "Un pequeño paraíso para crecer" + one-line offer (estimulación temprana, nido y cuidado · 0 a 3 años · Imperial, Cañete) + WhatsApp primary + "Recorre los espacios" secondary. Right 7/12: aerial patchwork of 5 plots — two large photo plots (sala de juego, rincón círculo), one photo plot (padres), two colour plots with labels. Acequia starts in the hero and runs down. Mobile: headline, then patchwork 2-col, then offer and CTAs (amended per the user's binding request that the first thing seen is the center's infrastructure); the floating and header WhatsApp buttons keep the action reachable above the fold.

FORM: Valle en parcelas, candidate 7 of 7 on the ordered list; seed key ca85ee5c. Raises: one module grid; layered drift for clouds/furrows with still reading plane; acequia drawn by SVG from live layout; water arrives at each plot in sequence on scroll.
