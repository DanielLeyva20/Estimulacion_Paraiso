# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Angular (user's explicit choice).

## Users

Parents (mostly mothers, also fathers and caregivers) of babies and toddlers aged 1–2 in Imperial, Cañete (Lima, Peru), usually browsing on a phone, often arriving from WhatsApp, Facebook or Instagram links. They are deciding whether this is a safe, warm, well-equipped place to leave or accompany their child.

## Product Purpose

Website for "Mi Paraíso", a small early-stimulation and childcare center in Imperial, Cañete. The site sells the experience first — the spaces, the atmosphere, the care — and only then invites booking. Success: a parent feels they have "visited" the center and writes on WhatsApp to ask or book.

## Positioning

A small, close, neighbourhood center in Imperial: personal attention in small groups, a space designed for 1–2 year olds, parents welcome.

## Operating Context

- Contact and booking happen exclusively through WhatsApp: +51 974 987 291 (wa.me/51974987291).
- No backend: the "Matricula a tu niño" form validates on the page and opens WhatsApp with the filled-in record (child's name, birth date and computed age, program of interest, guardian's name, DNI, mobile, Cañete district and address, plus consent).
- Spanish (Peru) is the only language.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Services:
- Estimulación temprana (1–2 años)
- Talleres / Nido
- Educación y cuidado de niños
- Vacaciones útiles: every year in January and February, ages 1–2
- Talleres personalizados (the director designs them): individual, small group, or with a parent

Form rules: names letters and spaces only, at least name and surname; DNI exactly 8 digits; Peruvian mobile 9 digits starting with 9; district chosen from the 16 districts of Cañete; child must be 1–2 years old.

Spaces to show:
- Sala de juego / arte
- Área para padres

Undecided: schedules, prices, exact address, staff names, social media handles. Do not invent them; leave clearly marked placeholders.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Mi Paraíso.
- Strong, vivid colors (director's request, 2026-10-29; replaces the earlier pastel requirement): turquoise, mustard, coral, steel blue, purple on a warm cream ground.
- Clean animations and effects that suit an early-stimulation center (user requirement).

## Evidence on Hand

No real photos yet: use reference/stock imagery, clearly replaceable. No testimonials, credentials, or statistics exist — do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Show the place before asking for anything: imagery of the spaces leads.
2. Calm and safety over excitement: parents of babies need reassurance.
3. One action: WhatsApp, always reachable.
4. Honest and local: no invented numbers or reviews.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Mobile-first; readable type sizes for tired parents on small phones; respect reduced-motion preferences.
